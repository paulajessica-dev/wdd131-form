Paradise Explorer - Site Plan

Site Name

Paradise Explorer

This name was chosen because it represents a travel website focused on tropical destinations and paradise beaches around the world.

Site Purpose

Paradise Explorer provides information about tropical destinations, weather conditions, countries, and beach galleries.

Scenarios

  • What are the best tropical beaches to visit?
  • What is the weather like at my destination?
