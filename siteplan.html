Paradise Explorer - Site Plan

Site Name

🌴 Paradise Explorer

This name was chosen because it represents a travel website focused on tropical destinations and paradise beaches around the world.

Site Purpose

Paradise Explorer is a travel website designed to help users discover tropical destinations around the world. The site provides destination guides, weather information, country facts, travel inspiration, and photo galleries to help travelers plan their next adventure.

Scenarios

  • What are the most beautiful tropical beaches to visit around the world?
  • What is the current weather like at my selected beach destination?
  • What country is this beach located in, and what are some interesting facts about it?
